Klaar takes raw laboratory bio-composite research and standardizes it into modular architectural components. The core of every product is Nero Materia: an unpigmented composite matrix achieved by saturating high-early-strength Portland cement ($CEM\ I\ 52.5R$) with post-consumer coffee grounds sourced at 0km from local corporate bars.

The coffee aggregate is oven-stabilized at 110°C to extract residual water before mixing, ensuring a fixed water-to-cement ratio ($\Sigma \ge 62\ MPa$ at $A/C\ 0.31$). This ensures certified structural integrity, deep matte carbon absorption, and an honest tactile feedback that resin plastic can never copy.

LAB DIARY — MIX MODIFICATION
LOG #025 — PARTICLE GRANULOMETRY AND HYDRODYNAMICS
Spent espresso grinds exhibit fluid-retention fluctuations that heavily impact early cementitious hydration. Granulometric curves vary dramatically based on origin. To counteract this, I've instituted a strict pre-pour thermal processing sequence: a uniform 110°C convection oven bake for exactly 45 minutes. Standardizing baseline aggregate humidity is mandatory for industrial batch replication. Without control, it's just an art hobby; with control, it becomes architectural technology.
12.05.2026
CRITICAL FAILURE ANALYSIS
LOG #022 — RESIN EXOTHERM AND THERMAL SHOCK CRACKING
Destroyed a specialized silicone-coated formwork block assembly during last night's pour. The two-part co-polymer technical resin layer was introduced before the cement core completed its curing phase. The chemical heat spiked rapidly to 68°C at the interface zone, causing acute differential thermal contraction. Result: deep cross-plane through-cracking and immediate mold adhesion failure. Lesson logged: full hydration curve termination must occur prior to resin glazing introduction. Failed data is our primary asset.
03.05.2026
MATERIAL PHILOSOPHY HYPOTHESIS
LOG #019 — STRUCTURAL BLACK VS. SURFACE COATING
Nero Materia explicitly refutes external dyes. The chromatic darkness is entirely mechanical: spent organic carbon crystals saturate the porous network until total visual absorption is achieved. It is an active black that swallows oncoming photon rays rather than returning them. I am currently mapping out the precise density threshold where the cured surface completely zero-registers ambient light refraction. It's a brutal, deep aesthetic state that cannot be duplicated by pigment coatings.
