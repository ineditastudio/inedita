24.05.2026
FIELD NOTE
THE FORMULA CHANGES AGAIN
The granulometry of coffee affects water retention more than I thought. Every bag of spent espresso has a different distribution. I've started baking the grounds in the oven at 110°C to standardise residual moisture before mixing. If it's not replicable, it's not a business — it's just an experiment.
12.05.2026
FAILURE REPORT
LOG #022 — THE EXOTHERM
Lost a €40 formwork last night. The resin reacted at 68°C in contact with the still-fresh cement. Result: irregular contraction, through-crack, stuck mould. Noted. Next time I'll wait for full set before the epoxy layer. Controlled failure is part of the programme.
03.05.2026
HYPOTHESIS
BLACK IS NOT A COLOUR
Nero Materia is not pigmented. The black comes from density — from the carbon of burnt coffee saturating the matrix. It's a black that absorbs, not one that reflects. I'm trying to find the optical threshold beyond which the surface stops registering light.
18.04.2026
STRATEGY · HALO EFFECT
THREE BRANDS, ONE STORY
The secret is not dividing the channels, but making them talk to each other. Studio builds authority, Klaar shows the process, Inedita creates the emotional bond. Every piece of content feeds all three. Don't advertise — curate. Attend events not as a seller, but as a circular economy expert.
05.04.2026
COLLABORATION · OLV LAB
ART IN RESIDENCE — FIRST SESSION
Workshop with three architecture students. We poured together, failed together. The formwork is a thinking tool, not just a shaping one. Three works displayed in a local bar — no commission. The owner, two weeks later, asked for a consultation to renovate the entrance. There it is: the €1,000 consulting job.
