ABOUT · INEDITA

BLACK MATTER.
SINGLE EDITION.

Inedita is the artistic soul of the system. Each work is born from the same research laboratory — the intersection of coffee extraction residues, structural cement and technical resins. The result is a material vocabulary that is impossible to replicate: the black is not applied, it is intrinsic. The texture is not designed, it is the consequence of the process.

Every piece carries a numbered archive code. Once sold, it leaves the laboratory and enters a collection. Two identical mixes on two different days produce different results. The material is the author.

Klaar is the scalable arm of the system. Where Inedita is singular and irreproducible, Klaar is repeatable — but never generic. The same Nero Materia formula that produces unique artworks is here applied to functional, desirable objects at accessible price points.

24.05.2026
FIELD NOTE
THE FORMULA CHANGES AGAIN
The granulometry of coffee affects water retention more than I thought. Every bag of spent espresso has a different distribution. I've started baking the grounds in the oven at 110°C to standardise residual moisture before mixing. If it's not replicable, it's not a business — it's just an experiment.
12.05.2026
FAILURE REPORT
LOG #022 — THE EXOTHERM
Lost a €40 formwork last night. The resin reacted at 68°C in contact with the still-fresh cement. Result: irregular contraction, through-crack, stuck mould. Noted. Next time I'll wait for full set before the epoxy layer. Controlled failure is part of the programme.
03.05.2026
HYPOTHESIS
BLACK IS NOT A COLOUR
Nero Materia is not pigmented. The black comes from density — from the carbon of burnt coffee saturating the matrix. It's a black that absorbs, not one that reflects. I'm trying to find the optical threshold beyond which the surface stops registering light.
18.04.2026
STRATEGY · HALO EFFECT
THREE BRANDS, ONE STORY
The secret is not dividing the channels, but making them talk to each other. Studio builds authority, Klaar shows the process, Inedita creates the emotional bond. Every piece of content feeds all three. Don't advertise — curate.
05.04.2026
COLLABORATION · OLV LAB
ART IN RESIDENCE — FIRST SESSION
Workshop with three architecture students. We poured together, failed together. The formwork is a thinking tool, not just a shaping one. Three works in a local bar — no commission. The owner, two weeks later, asked for a consultation to renovate the entrance.
