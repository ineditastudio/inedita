24.05.2026
FIELD NOTE
THE FORMULA CHANGES AGAIN
The granulometry of coffee affects water retention more than I thought. Every bag of spent espresso has a different distribution. I've started baking the grounds in the oven at 110°C to standardise residual moisture before mixing. If it is not replicable, it is not a business — it is just an experiment.
12.05.2026
FAILURE REPORT
LOG #022 — THE EXOTHERM
Lost a €40 formwork last night. The resin reacted at 68°C in contact with still-fresh cement. Result: irregular contraction, through-crack, stuck mould. Noted. Next time I will wait for full set before the epoxy layer. Controlled failure is part of the programme — you only learn the material by pushing it wrong.
03.05.2026
HYPOTHESIS
BLACK IS NOT A COLOUR
Nero Materia is not pigmented. The black comes from density — from the carbon of burnt coffee saturating the matrix. It is a black that absorbs, not one that reflects. I am trying to find the optical threshold beyond which the surface stops registering light. There might not be one. That would be interesting.
18.04.2026
